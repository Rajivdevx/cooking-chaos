# Cooking Chaos

**Developed by Rajivdevx**

Cooking Chaos is a fast-paced, grid-based kitchen management game where you chop, cook, fry, and deliver food to beat the clock!

## 🎮 How to Play

*   **Move:** Use `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` or the **Arrow Keys** to move around the kitchen.
*   **Interact:** Press `E` to pick up ingredients, place them on counters, or deliver completed dishes to the window.
*   **Chop:** Hold `F` at a cutting board to chop ingredients (Tomatoes, Cabbage, Potatoes).

### The Stations
*   **Pantry:** Pick up fresh ingredients like Tomatoes 🍅, Cabbage 🥬, Potatoes 🥔, Meat 🥩, Cheese 🧀, and Bread 🍞.
*   **Cutting Board (Bottom Left):** Place Tomatoes, Cabbage, or Potatoes here and hold `F` to chop them into 🥣🍅, 🥣🥬, or 🔪🥔.
*   **Stove (Bottom Right):** Place Meat here and wait for it to cook (🍖). Be careful not to burn it (🍖🔥)!
*   **Fryer (Bottom Right):** Place Chopped Potatoes (🔪🥔) here to fry them into Fries (🍟). Don't burn them (🍟🔥)!
*   **Assembly Counters (Top Center):** Combine cooked and chopped ingredients to create the required dishes (Salads and Burgers).
*   **Delivery Window (Top Center):** Drop off completed orders here to score points!
*   **Trash Bin (Bottom Right):** Toss out ruined or burnt food.

## 🚀 Game Modes

*   **🏆 Challenge Mode:** Score 500 points before the time runs out! Keep an eye on the clock.
*   **♾️ Endless Mode:** No timer. Cook forever and see how high of a score you can achieve!

## ⚙️ Features
*   **Dynamic Audio:** Separate volume controls for background music and sound effects.
*   **Replay & Pause:** Instantly pause the game or restart a run directly from the top HUD.
*   **Custom Difficulty:** Choose between Easy, Normal, or Hard to adjust how fast orders come in.

## 💻 Tech Stack
*   React 19 + TypeScript
*   Vite
*   CSS Modules / Inline Styling
*   Web Audio API for dynamic sound effects and synthesized tones
